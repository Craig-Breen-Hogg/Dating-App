Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

Introduction

harrys ("we", "us", "our") operates a dating app built around shared activities instead of swiping. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Account information: your name, email address, date of birth (used to calculate and display your age), and gender.

Profile content: photos and videos you upload, the hobbies and interests you select, and any bio or preference details you add.

Location data: your approximate location, used to find nearby matches and suggest venues. You can decline location access, but doing so limits matching.

Activity data: the matches ("Drops") you receive, messages you send in your matches, activities you propose, and purchases you make.

Safety data: reports you submit, users you block, and reports submitted about you.

Device and log data: basic technical information such as app version and crash logs, used to keep the service running reliably.

How We Use Your Information

To generate your weekly curated matches based on shared hobbies, preferences, and proximity.

To operate messaging between matched users.

To review reports, enforce blocks, and keep the community safe.

To process purchases such as the Midweek Activity Pass.

To maintain and improve the app, including diagnosing technical issues.

Photos and Location

Photos and videos you upload are visible to users you are matched with. You can remove them at any time from your Profile.

Location is used only to calculate approximate distance for matching and venue suggestions — your exact coordinates are never shown to other users.

How We Share Your Information

With other users: your profile (name, age, photos, hobbies, and approximate distance) is shown to users you are matched with. Messages are visible only to the two people in a match.

With service providers: we use Supabase for database hosting, authentication, and file storage, and app store platforms (Apple App Store, Google Play) for processing in-app purchases. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security obligations.

For legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, or to investigate violations of our Terms of Service or protect the safety of our users.

We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile, photos, matches, and messages are permanently deleted — see "Account Deletion" below.

Your Rights and Choices

You can review and edit your profile information, preferences, and photos at any time from the Profile tab.

You can block or unblock other users, and report content or behavior that violates our Terms of Service.

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to correct inaccurate data by contacting us at the address below.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account. This immediately and irreversibly deletes your profile, photos, matches, messages, and login — nothing is retained.

Children's Privacy

harrys is only for people 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

Data Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit and row-level access controls on our database, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email us at privacy@harrysapp.com.
